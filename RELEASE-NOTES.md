# Kane & Lynch 2 VR 0.6.41

## Controls and gameplay

- **A** reloads when tapped with the movement stick centred. Hold **A** while moving to sprint. Releasing **A** early during movement leaves the magazine in place.
- Magazine pickup and insertion accept slightly more room near the belt and the magazine well. Adjusted forearm and finger motion and improved canister and cylinder throws.
- **B** uses the same get-up action when wounded near cover. Hold **B** near a low obstacle to vault. Arcade helicopter escape prompts show VR buttons.

## HUD and rendering

- Subtitles, speaker names and icons shift together toward the centre. Native crosshair proportions are preserved in VR, and the laser and its impact dot track more consistently.
- The settings window keeps the controls players need. Experimental VR cutscenes are off by default.
- Supported gameplay draws both native eye views from one game update on a fresh installation. Rendering reuses more visibility, skeleton and HUD work across the two eyes. Frame rates still vary across scenes, headsets and renderers.

## Updating

Close the game and the old settings window, then run **KaneLynch2VR-0.6.41-Setup.exe**. A portable ZIP and SHA-256 checksums are also available. Existing settings are preserved when updating.

Rare Steam crashes, stutter and missing surfaces remain possible. The mod targets Windows Steam single-player. See the [installation guide](INSTALLATION.md), [controls](CONTROLS.md) and [known issues](KNOWN-ISSUES.md).
